# ADR-033: Allocation plans are mutable; receipt and variance evidence is immutable

An open `FactoryArrival` may split each immutable source row across warehouses, including a single quantity such as 30 into 20 + 10. Quantities already received or closed as shortage are fixed; only the remainder can move. One request may partially receive multiple rows and warehouses atomically. Normal receipt reduces `incoming` and increases `onHand`; overage increases `onHand` without increasing expected quantity and requires a reason linked to the source arrival. A shortage closure removes the remaining expectation with a reason. Later goods are recorded as a closure-linked follow-up arrival/receipt without reopening the original expected quantity. A correction never edits evidence: it creates an immutable full-line reversal and compensating inventory transaction. Request identifiers and database-derived payload identity make retries idempotent.

Allocation changes must retain before/after warehouse quantities, actor, time, and reason. Receipt evidence retains the user-editable business receipt date separately from processing time. Terminal arrivals can be explicitly closed, while actual received quantities remain visible in inventory immediately during partial status.
